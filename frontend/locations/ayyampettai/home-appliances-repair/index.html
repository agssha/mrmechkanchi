Our service engineers are certified to troubleshoot and repair a diverse range of mechanical and electronic hardware issues:

  • Mixer Grinder Motor & Coupler Jam: Overloading or worn-out jar couplers prevent the blades from rotating, or causes the motor to spark and shut down.
